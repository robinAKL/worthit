Please identify which organisation can grant the relevant rights, including any participating store approval needed; we would not assume that approval for one region, banner or store covers another.

The proposed fields are product identity, price, pack size, supplied unit price, factual specifications, availability, source URL and update time. We would preserve the applicable store, channel, member or promotion conditions and avoid presenting a local price as a national offer. Each result would identify its store, link to its original page and show the check time. Images, original descriptions, reviews and customer data are excluded.

An official feed would be preferable. For any approved page access, we would agree an identifiable client, allowed paths and refresh limits before starting, follow applicable robots rules and your instructions, and avoid accounts, search and checkout. Please also specify permitted retention, attribution, corrections and withdrawal arrangements so we can implement them before a pilot.

We are open to discussing a fee per click or a fixed monthly fee, subject to a separate agreement. We do not promise traffic or assume an existing commission programme. Paid relationships would be disclosed and kept separate from the recommendation criteria.
