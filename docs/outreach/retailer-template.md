Images, original descriptions, reviews and customer data are outside this request.

We are open to discussing a fee per click or a fixed monthly fee, subject to a separate agreement. There is no committed budget or traffic forecast. We would disclose paid relationships and keep the explanation of a product's ranking independent of payment.
