Robin reviews, completes and sends these drafts personally. Agents do not send them or contact the retailers. No request has been sent, and no permission is recorded here.

## English (to send)

**Subject:** Permission to show your product images on WorthIt, a New Zealand price comparison site

Kia ora [name or team],

I'm [Robin's full name], and I'm building WorthIt ([project URL]), a New Zealand site that helps shoppers see whether a price is good. It compares each item with similar items, for example price per litre for fridges, and links back to the retailer's own product page to buy.

[Ascent / Baby Factory] products already appear on WorthIt with their price and key specifications, each linked to your product page. Right now we show a plain specification panel in place of a photo, because we don't use anyone's images without permission.

We'd like your written permission to show **one main product image per item**. If you agree, we will:

- show only the image you already publish as the main product image, displayed from the image address on your own site; we would not download, edit or keep copies of your image files;
- link every image and card to the product page on [ascent.co.nz / babyfactory.co.nz];
- store only that image's address, and drop it when the product changes or leaves your site;
- stop showing all of your images within [7] days of a request, for any reason;
- not use your images in advertising, social media or anywhere outside the product listing.

If you have a product feed or an affiliate programme that already licenses images, we'd be happy to use that instead. Please point us to the right contact.

A short reply confirming permission, from someone able to grant it, is enough. Please also let us know if you'd like any credit line or other conditions.
